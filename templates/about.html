📡 AI Privacy & Surveillance

Our FallGuard system is built on the principle of "Privacy by Design." Instead of streaming traditional video which could compromise patient dignity, our AI engine processes visual data locally to extract motion patterns.

Skeletal Masking

Uses MediaPipe to extract 30+ key body landmarks. The system only "sees" points and lines, never the person's actual image.

CNN + LSTM Model

A hybrid Deep Learning architecture that analyzes temporal sequences to differentiate between a fall and normal daily activities (ADLs).

Why it matters?

In hospital environments, rapid response is the difference between recovery and permanent injury. By automating the monitoring process, we provide 24/7 coverage without the need for constant human supervision of private rooms.
