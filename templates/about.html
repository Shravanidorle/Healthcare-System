AI Privacy & Surveillance

Our FallGuard system is built on the principle of "Privacy by Design." Instead of streaming traditional video which could compromise patient dignity, our AI engine processes visual data locally to extract abstract motion patterns securely.

Skeletal Masking

Advanced computer vision utilizes MediaPipe to extract 30+ key body landmarks in real-time. The system only "sees" points and lines, guaranteeing the patient's actual image is never stored or transmitted.

This approach converts potentially invasive camera feeds into completely anonymous data streams that are impossible to reverse-engineer into human likenesses.

CNN + LSTM Model

A hybrid Deep Learning architecture powers the core decision engine. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) extract spatial features while Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) layers analyze the temporal sequence.

This allows the AI to accurately differentiate between a critical fall and normal daily activities (ADLs) over a rolling 30-frame window.

Why it matters?

In hospital environments, rapid response is the difference between recovery and permanent injury. By automating the monitoring process securely, we provide 24/7 proactive coverage without the need for constant, invasive human supervision of private rooms.
